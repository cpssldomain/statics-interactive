Procedure (Sections 4.4–4.5)

  1. Position vector r: from the moment point O (or from any point on the axis a) to any point on the line of action of F. Pick the point that makes r simplest.
  2. Force vector F: if F is given by a magnitude and a line, write F = F u, with u = rAB/rAB along the line.
  3. MO = r × F: expand the determinant |i j k; rx ry rz; Fx Fy Fz|. Order matters: F × r = −(r × F).
  4. Several forces: (MR)O = Σ(r × F).
  5. About an axis: Ma = ua · (r × F). A positive Ma acts along ua, a negative one opposite to it. As a vector, Ma = Maua.
  6. Scalar shortcut (axes x, y, z): Mx, My, Mz = Σ ± F d, right-hand rule for each sign. A force parallel to an axis, or through it, has no moment about it.
Based on Hibbeler, Engineering Mechanics: Statics, 16th ed., Sections 4.4–4.5. Answers given to three significant figures, as in the book.
