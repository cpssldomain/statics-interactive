6. Class challenge

Predict one unknown member force before revealing the solution.

Use a negative value for compression. Assessment checks the actual solved equilibrium, not a visual estimate.

Assumptions: pin-connected, two-force members; concentrated forces applied at joints; no member self-weight or distributed member loads; planar statically determinate geometry with one pin and one vertical roller. Whole-truss reactions follow ∑Fx = 0, ∑Fy = 0, and ∑MA = 0. A member is taken as tensile when its computed force is positive. The member-count condition is necessary, but not sufficient, for stability in general trusses.
