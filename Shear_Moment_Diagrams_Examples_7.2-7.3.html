Step 2

Shear and moment

Choose a region, move the cut inside it, and write ΣFy = 0 and ΣMS = 0 for the left segment. Then compare your V(x) and M(x) with the functions.

Build the diagrams from left to right using: slope of V = w, ΔV = area under w, jumps of V at forces; slope of M = V, ΔM = area under V, jumps of M at couples. Each step reveals the diagrams up to that point.

Fill in the shear just left (⁻) and just right (⁺) of each key point, and the moment there. Where a value does not apply, the box is not shown.
